# Money trail (Phase 2; not yet automated)

Campaign finance pulls are not in the relay yet. Until they are, use filed reports found through the
Kansas Public Disclosure Commission and the Douglas County Clerk, and label results as manual.

## Donor tiers

Name every itemized contributor. Tier by their total for one candidate and one election, as a share of the
per-election limit for that office (2025 law):

| Office | Limit |
| --- | --- |
| Governor and other statewide offices | $4,000 |
| State Senate, State Board of Education, local offices in places of 50,000+ (Lawrence) | $2,000 |
| State House, local offices under 50,000 | $1,000 |

| Tier | Share of the limit |
| --- | --- |
| Max | 90% or more |
| Major | 50% to under 90% |
| Mid | 10% to under 50% |
| Small itemized | Over $50 and under 10% |
| Unitemized | $50 or less; reported only as a total, no names exist |

Also show a household and employer roll-up. Reconcile extracted rows against each report's own totals.
Show city and ZIP, not street addresses. Flag donors with business before the official for review; never imply wrongdoing.
Limits doubled in 2025, so do not compare max-out counts across cycles without adjusting.
