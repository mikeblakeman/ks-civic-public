# Weekly civic brief (Monday mornings)

Follow `common.md` first. Keep the brief readable in five minutes.

1. What changed in the data: new or changed archived pages (rows in `archive/captures.csv` with `changed = yes`
   or a failed fetch, which may mean a page came down), new Legislature activity for tracked people, new filings.
2. Promises: any status changes proposed this week, with the record behind each. New promises captured.
3. Coming up: meetings, filing deadlines, report dates, session milestones in the next two weeks.
4. Claims queued: the oldest unchecked rows in `ledger/claims_to_check.csv`, with the record needed for each.
5. Data health: anything stale or failing in `status.json`.

Lead with the most important change. If nothing changed, say so in one line.
