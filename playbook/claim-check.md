# Claim check

Use when Mike states a conclusion, or for any row in `ledger/claims_to_check.csv`.

## Verdict first

Supported, Partly supported, Not supported, Contradicted, or Can't tell yet. Then: evidence for, evidence against,
gaps, and what data would change the verdict. Do not soften a verdict to be agreeable.

## Checks, every time

- Attribution: one person's act, or a body's vote?
- Authority: could they do it alone?
- Real terms: inflation (CPI-U) and per-capita adjustments before calling anything growth or a cut.
- Full window: the whole series, not the years that make the point.
- Comparison: compared with what?
- Cause: a donation followed by a favorable vote is a lead, not proof.
- Missing data: unitemized gifts, unscanned reports, data-entry lag, gaps in meeting video.
- The other side: the strongest case from the same records.

## Recording

For ledger claims, propose the verdict and evidence to Mike in chat. The relay row changes only with his approval.
