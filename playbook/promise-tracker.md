# Updating promise statuses

1. Start from `ledger/promises.csv` (or the `promises` table in `civic.db`).
2. For each promise with a measurable test, look for the primary record that would satisfy it:
   bill actions and roll calls (`data/kslegis`), governor actions, signed orders, court dockets, budget lines, agency reports.
3. Score effort and outcome separately. One official cannot deliver a shared-authority promise alone.
4. Change a status only with a primary record in hand. Record the evidence in `notes` with a link,
   and propose the change to Mike in chat rather than editing silently.
5. A second pass reviews every proposed change using only the promise and the evidence, not the first pass's reasoning.
   If the passes disagree, the status stays In progress or Unverifiable.
6. Never describe an official as good, bad, or failing. Report the record.
